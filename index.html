Who I Am

Before data, there was language. I started out studying English at Ibn Zohr University, then picked up code on the side through ALX Africa's Software Engineering program. That combination is what pulled me toward a Master's in Data and Discourse Studies at TU Darmstadt — a program built around treating data the way I used to treat text: something with structure worth digging into.

Day to day, that splits into two sides: HTML, CSS and JavaScript for building things, and Python, SQL, Excel and Power BI for cleaning a messy dataset and turning what's left into something a non-technical reader can act on. I'm looking for a data analytics internship where that combination is useful — someone who can build the pipeline and also explain what it found.
